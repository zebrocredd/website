Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum loan amount I can get?
You can apply for a loan amount ranging from ₹10,000 up to ₹10 lakhs depending on your eligibility and repayment capacity.
How long does it take to get loan approval?
Most applications are approved within 24 hours, and funds are disbursed immediately after verification.
Do I need to provide collateral?
No. Our personal and other loans are completely unsecured — no collateral or guarantor required.
Can I prepay or foreclose my loan?
Yes, you can prepay your loan anytime after 6 months. Minimal foreclosure charges may apply as per terms.
What documents are required?
You’ll need to submit your PAN card, Aadhaar, address proof, and income documents such as salary slips or bank statements.
